Agent-Guided Policy Repair for Autonomous Driving

Secure Reasoning Lab, NYU  ·  with Prof. Jiahao Yu  ·  2026–present

A language-model agent reads a failed simulated drive and proposes an earlier action that would have changed the outcome; verified repairs are then used as training signal for the driving policy.

Closed-Loop Motion Planning in Roadway Work Zones

biLAB, NYU Tandon  ·  with Prof. Semiha Ergan  ·  2026

A configurable CARLA–SUMO testbed comprising a normal-traffic work zone and five safety-critical scenario families, demonstrated with a jointly trained PPO planner and structured scene encoder. Scenario-wise evaluation characterizes planner capabilities, failure modes, and within-town transfer limitations.
